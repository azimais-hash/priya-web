h2: About the event
p: Becoming a mum changes everything - your body, your routine, your relationships, your sense of self. But while everyone focuses on the baby, who’s looking after you ?
p: So much of the overwhelm in motherhood doesn’t come from doing things ‘wrong’, it comes from all the expectations around us. What we’re told things should look like.
p: What we see on social media.
p: What we hear from others, from books, from advice coming at us from every direction.
p: It can start to feel confusing.
p: Like there’s a right way you’re meant to be parenting, and you’re constantly checking yourself against it.
