h2: What does coaching actually feel like?
span: Lets have a conversation
p: Think about a backpack you've been carrying for years.
p: At first, you didn't even notice it. You simply adjusted to the weight. Responsibilities, expectations, other people's needs, old beliefs about who you 'should' be, they were added gradually, and you got stronger at carrying them. You kept walking.
p: But over time, the weight becomes normal. The tight shoulders, the quiet exhaustion, the sense that everything feels heavier than it should, that just becomes your life.
p: Coaching offers a rare moment to stop walking and gently take the backpack off.
p: You get to open it. To look inside. To notice what you've been carrying - the guilt, the pressure, the fear of disappointing others, the stories you've been telling yourself.
p: Some things still belong to you. Some were never yours to carry. And others you're finally ready to put down.
p: I don't empty the bag for you. I don't decide what stays or goes.
p: I simply sit beside you while you sort through it.
p: When you stand up again, the road hasn't changed.
p: But you feel lighter, and that changes everything.
