About the event

Becoming a mum changes everything - your body, your routine, your relationships, your sense of self. But while everyone focuses on the baby, who’s looking after you?




So much of the overwhelm in motherhood doesn’t come from doing things ‘wrong’, it comes from all the expectations around us. What we’re told things should look like.

What we see on social media.

 What we hear from others, from books, from advice coming at us from every direction.

It can start to feel confusing.




 Like there’s a right way you’re meant to be parenting, and you’re constantly checking yourself against it.

But the truth is, there is no one right way.




And there is nothing wrong with you.

You just need space to listen to yourself and that’s something you’re allowed to have.

What often gets lost in all that noise is your own inner knowing, your motherly instinct, your sense of what feels right for you and your baby.

The work I do with mums is about helping you slow things down enough to trust yourself again.

To notice what matters to you.

To give yourself some grace.

To understand your own values, needs, and limits, so you can parent in a way that feels aligned, not pressured or confusing.

When you trust yourself more, everything feels a little lighter.

Decisions become clearer.

The noise quietens.

And you can meet motherhood from a place that feels more grounded and more like you.




A Mindful Journey for New Mums is a nurturing 6-week in-person group designed to help you feel grounded, seen, and supported as you navigate early motherhood.




✨ Each week blends:

Mindfulness practices for calm & connection

Gentle group coaching reflections

Tools to help with overwhelm, exhaustion, and mum guilt

Journaling prompts, affirmations, and soothing takeaways




No pressure. No performance. Just real conversations and restorative moments - for you, with baby warmly welcomed.




Who’s it for: Mums with babies (non-crawling babies welcome to join)
