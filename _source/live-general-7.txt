THE RECLAMATION PROGRAMME™

You’ve built the life. Now find yourself inside it.

 

A private, six-month coaching advisory for high-achieving women navigating an identity transition. Rigorous, intelligent, deeply human.

​

12 sessions  ·  2 per month  ·  6 months  ·  1:1 private

Before you read another word, hear it from me directly.
 

The work I do with women is private and it is personal. No summary on a web page will do it justice, so I’d like to introduce myself properly.

You’ve done everything right. So why does it feel like it’s no longer quite yours?

You’re good at what you do, you show up for your work, your family, your team, your community. You hold a great deal together, usually without anyone realising quite how much.

However, underneath the competence, something has shifted, quietly, gradually, without a single dramatic moment you could point to. A faint sense that the life you’ve built,the one that looks right from the outside, doesn’t quite fit the woman you are now.

​

It's not crisis, it doesn't feel like burnout or ingratitude, but something harder to name than that.

 

“Maybe having a fulfilled life, but not feeling fulfilled within it.”

 

You might recognise this:

 

✔︎  Successful on paper, but privately questioning whether this is still what you want

✔︎  Exhausted in a way that sleep and holidays don’t seem to fix

✔︎  So good at being who everyone needs you to be that you’ve lost the thread back to yourself

✔︎  Pulled between your roles, your responsibilities, and a quiet longing for something that feels more genuinely yours

✔︎  Wondering, privately, carefully - who you are on the other side of all of this

 

If you nodded at even one of those - you’re in exactly the right place.

​

What you’re experiencing has a name. It’s identity displacement.

Over years, sometimes decades, of building a life around other people’s needs, expectations, and definitions of success, the person who was doing all of that building quietly stepped aside. A highly competent performance took her place and for a long time, nobody noticed, including you.

​

The roles weren’t wrong. The achievements weren’t wrong, but somewhere along the way, the life stopped feeling authored by you.

WHY THIS MATTERS

​

This isn’t a problem you can think your way out of, or solve with a new plan, a promotion, or a holiday. It requires something different: a structured, intelligent, deeply personal process of returning to yourself.  Space to recognise what has been yours all along, releasing what never was, and redesigning your life from the inside out.

Empower Your Journey
