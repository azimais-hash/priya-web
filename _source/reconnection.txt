h1: You've built a life that looks right.  So why does it feel like it no longer fits?
h6: Something doesn't fit - but you can't quite name it yet.
p: When did you last sit with a question that was just about you?
p: Not your role, or your responsibilities. Definitely not what needs to be done or who needs you next.
p: Just you - what you're feeling, what's been quietly building, what you haven't quite had the words for yet.
p: Most women never do, not because they don't want to. but because they've never felt they had permission.
p: The Reconnection is that permission.
p: ​
p: A private self-reflection of 12 questions, around 15 minutes,  created entirely for you. Not to produce anything, not to reach a conclusion. To finally hear yourself think, without the noise of everything and everyone else.
p: ​
p: There are no right answers, no scores, no categories. Nothing to perform and no one to reassure.
p: Just an honest conversation with yourself, held in a space that belongs entirely to you.
p: Once you've completed it, I'll read everything you've shared and write back to you personally within 72 hours.
p: Not a template, a response that reflects what I see in your words, what's present, what's possible, what you might not yet have been able to say out loud.
p: ​
p: This is not a sales pitch. It's the beginning of a conversation.
p: This is for you if you paused at that first question and felt something.
