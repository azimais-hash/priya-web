## About the event

Becoming a mum changes everything - your body, your routine, your relationships, your sense of self. But while everyone focuses on the baby, who's looking after you?

A Mindful Journey for New Mums is a nurturing 6-week in-person group designed to help you feel grounded, seen, and supported as you navigate early motherhood.

Each week blends:

<ul class="ticks">
<li>Mindfulness practices for calm &amp; connection</li>
<li>Gentle group coaching reflections</li>
<li>Tools to help with overwhelm, exhaustion, and mum guilt</li>
<li>Baby-inclusive moments (eye-gazing, rocking, bonding)</li>
<li>Journaling prompts, affirmations, and soothing takeaways</li>
</ul>

<p class="big">No pressure. No performance. Just real conversations and restorative moments - for you, with baby warmly welcomed.</p>

<ul class="facts">
<li><span>Who it's for</span>Mums with babies (pre-crawling ideally), 0-12 months</li>

<li><span>Bonus</span>Whatsapp group support and Reflection Journal</li>
</ul>
