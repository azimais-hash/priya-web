## About the event

<p class="eyebrow">Introducing: Rooted &amp; Rising</p>

A 6-week guided coaching programme for South Asian British-born mothers who are ready to:

<ul class="ticks">
<li>Let go of guilt passed down through generations</li>
<li>Understand and break cultural emotional patterns</li>
<li>Reconnect to their true identity</li>
<li>Set boundaries without shame</li>
<li>Mother from calm, clarity, and conscious choice</li>
</ul>

All while being seen, supported, and celebrated by women who get it.
