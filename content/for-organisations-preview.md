<p class="eyebrow">The Mindfully Priya difference</p>
<h2>More than a moment of calm</h2>
<p>I believe workplace wellbeing is about more than taking a break from a busy day. It's about helping people understand themselves better: how they respond to pressure, what affects their confidence, what matters to them and how they show up for themselves and others.</p>
<p>My approach combines professional coaching with mindfulness and practical reflection. I create space for people to pause, get curious and see things from a different perspective, before exploring what they might want to do differently.</p>
<p>Whether I'm working with an individual, a leadership team or a room full of employees, my aim is the same: for people to leave feeling heard, with greater awareness and something useful they can take into everyday life.</p>
<p>I come from the corporate world myself, so I understand the pressures your people are under.</p>

<p class="eyebrow">Specialist areas</p>
<h2>Supporting people through important transitions</h2>
<p>Alongside broader workplace wellbeing and leadership work, I bring particular experience supporting people through transitions that can affect confidence, identity and working life.</p>
<ul class="ticks">
<li><strong>Midlife and career transitions</strong>, including menopause at work and changes in personal or professional identity.</li>
<li><strong>Returning to work</strong> after maternity leave or another extended absence.</li>
<li><strong>Stepping into leadership</strong>, changing career or rethinking what sustainable success looks like.</li>
</ul>

<details class="more">
<summary>Coaching for maternity returners</summary>
<p>Coming back after maternity leave is a pivotal moment for the person and for the organisation. Without support, capable people can lose confidence and question their place long before they say anything.</p>
<p>I work with returners before and after the return: what they want this phase of their career to look like, how they hold their professional identity alongside a new one, and how they handle the first demanding months with some steadiness. Nine sessions is my usual recommendation, four before the return and four or five afterwards.</p>
</details>

<details class="more" style="margin-top:0;border-top:0">
<summary>About the Energy Leadership Index</summary>
<p>The Energy Leadership Index (ELI) is an attitudinal self-assessment, available through iPEC-certified coaches. It looks at how someone tends to respond to everyday situations and how that shifts under pressure. It is a tool for reflection and is not a diagnosis or a measure of performance.</p>
<p>It takes about 20 minutes online, followed by a 60 to 90 minute personal debrief with me and an action plan. Organisations use it for individual leaders, for whole teams who want shared language, and as a 360 that adds colleagues' perspectives.</p>
</details>
