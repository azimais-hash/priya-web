<p class="eyebrow">My approach</p>
<h2>A different way to think, feel and respond</h2>
<div class="pillars">
<div><h3>Professional coaching</h3><p>Thoughtful questions that help people see their situation clearly and decide what they want to do about it.</p></div>
<div><h3>Practical mindfulness</h3><p>Simple, everyday ways to notice what is happening in the moment and choose how to respond.</p></div>
<div><h3>Energy Leadership</h3><p>A framework for understanding the attitudes and patterns that shape how we lead, work and relate to others.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="approach-grid">
<div>
<h3>What happens in a session</h3>
<p>My sessions are warm, practical, interactive and reflective. Depending on the group, they may include coaching questions, discussion, guided mindfulness, personal reflection and practical exercises.</p>
<p>Everything is adapted to the audience, and nobody is ever pressured to share anything personal.</p>
</div>
<div class="eli">
<p class="eli-q">How do you show up when the pressure is on?</p>
<p>The Energy Leadership Index (ELI) is an attitudinal assessment that helps people explore their perspectives, reactions and patterns, particularly under stress. For managers, leaders and anyone navigating a challenge, it offers a clear, practical starting point for choosing how they want to respond.</p>
<p class="eli-note">The ELI is a specialist tool I use where it fits, usually in coaching and leadership work. It isn't part of every workshop.</p>
</div>
</div>
