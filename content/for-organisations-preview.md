<p class="eyebrow">The human side of work</p>
<h2>Looking after people isn't always straightforward</h2>
</div>
<div>
<p>Every organisation has different priorities, yet many of the human challenges are surprisingly familiar. You may recognise some of these.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="wrap">
<div class="specialisms" style="margin-top:2rem">
<div>
<h3>Managers need support too</h3>
<p>Managers are often expected to support everyone else, without always having support themselves. They carry difficult conversations, other people's worries and their own workload, often with little reassurance.</p>
</div>
<div>
<h3>Pressure and overwhelm</h3>
<p>Busy minds, competing demands and little space to reflect before reacting.</p>
</div>
<div>
<h3>Navigating change</h3>
<p>New roles, restructures, returning to work and shifting expectations can unsettle even the most capable people.</p>
</div>
<div>
<h3>Connection and confidence</h3>
<p>People do their best work when they feel heard, valued and confident enough to contribute.</p>
</div>
</div>
<p class="big" style="margin-top:3rem;max-width:46rem">Not every challenge needs another policy or presentation. Sometimes people need space, useful tools and a fresh perspective, so they can respond more consciously and move forward.</p>
</div>
</section>

<p class="eyebrow">Meet Priya</p>
<h2>More than a moment of calm</h2>
<p>I'm Priya Khan, an ICF-accredited and iPEC-certified coach, Energy Leadership Index Master Practitioner and mindfulness practitioner. Before coaching, I spent years in the corporate world, so I know first-hand the pressure people carry at work, and what it costs in energy, confidence and clarity.</p>
<p>I believe workplace wellbeing is about more than taking a break from a busy day. It's about helping people understand themselves better: how they respond to pressure, what affects their confidence and how they show up for themselves and others.</p>
<p>My approach blends professional coaching with mindfulness and practical reflection. Whether I'm working with one person or a room full of employees, my aim is the same: for people to leave feeling heard, with greater awareness and something useful they can take into everyday life.</p>

<div><p class="eyebrow">Questions</p><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2></div>
<div class="faq">
<details><summary>Can you deliver sessions online?</summary><p>Yes. I work online with teams and individuals throughout the UK, and offer in-person delivery by arrangement, particularly in South London and Croydon.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can we start with one wellbeing session?</summary><p>Absolutely. A single interactive workshop is the most common starting point. We can explore a series if ongoing support would be more valuable.</p></details>
<details><summary>Do you tailor sessions?</summary><p>Yes. We begin with your priorities and the people attending, then agree a suitable focus, format and level of interaction.</p></details>
<details><summary>Is individual coaching confidential?</summary><p>Yes, within clearly agreed professional and legal limits. The organisation and the participant agree the purpose of the coaching and any reporting arrangements in advance. What someone shares in a session is not passed to their employer.</p></details>
<details><summary>Is this therapy?</summary><p>No. Coaching and mindfulness are not therapy or medical treatment. If something a participant brings would be better supported elsewhere, I will say so and point them towards the right help.</p></details>
<details><summary>How much does it cost?</summary><p>It depends on group size, duration, preparation and whether the work is online or in person. The introductory conversation is free, and afterwards I'll send you a tailored proposal.</p></details>
