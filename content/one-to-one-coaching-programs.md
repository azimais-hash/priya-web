The main way I work is [The Reclamation Programme](/reclamation), six months of private coaching for women at a significant crossroads. What follows are the other ways in, for women who want something shorter or different.

After years of navigating the fast-paced corporate world while raising two children, I found myself overwhelmed and searching for balance. It was through mindfulness that I discovered a path to calm and emotional awareness, for my children and for myself. I'm here to help women reconnect with themselves, restore balance, and cultivate patience and joy through [mindful coaching](/mindfully-priya-coaching).

<p class="big">When was the last time someone asked how you really are and waited for the honest answer?</p>

My 1:1 coaching gives you an hour that belongs entirely to you. Somewhere to pause, breathe, and finally tune in to what you need.

Whether you're feeling overwhelmed, questioning your identity, or simply craving more balance and joy in your life, this is your invitation to come back to yourself, with gentleness, with compassion, and without guilt.

Using a blend of mindfulness, energy leadership coaching and reflection, I'll support you in shifting old patterns, putting down what was never yours to carry, and recognising the woman underneath all of it.
