I am Priya Khan, trading as Mindfully Priya, based in South London. I am the data
controller for this website. You can reach me at
[hello@mindfullypriya.com](mailto:hello@mindfullypriya.com) about anything on this page.

## What I collect, and why

**The Reconnection.** The twelve questions are hosted on [Tally](https://tally.so),
and your answers are stored there. I read them myself and write back to you
personally. Nobody else sees them. I do not use what you write in my articles,
my teaching or my marketing, and I do not add you to a mailing list because you
completed it.

**Booking a call.** The calendar is [Cal.com](https://cal.com). When you book, it
holds your name, email address, time zone and anything you write in the booking
form, and it puts the appointment in my calendar. I use it to prepare for our
conversation and to send you the link.

**Email.** If you email me, I keep the correspondence so I can pick up where we
left off.

**Visits to the site.** The site uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page
views without cookies and without building a profile of you. It does not identify
you and I cannot use it to see what one particular person read.

## How long I keep it

Enquiries and Reconnection responses: two years from our last contact, unless you
ask me to delete them sooner. Client records: six years after the work ends, which
is what UK tax rules require of me.

## Who else sees it

Only the services that make the site work: Tally for forms, Cal.com for booking,
Vercel for hosting, and my own email provider. I do not sell anything to anyone,
and I do not share your information for advertising.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask me for a copy of what I hold about you, ask me to
correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email me and I will do it, normally within a
few days and always within a month. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to
the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## Coaching is not therapy

Nothing on this site is therapy, counselling or medical treatment. If you are
struggling with your mental health, please speak to your GP. In an emergency, call
999 or the Samaritans on 116 123.
