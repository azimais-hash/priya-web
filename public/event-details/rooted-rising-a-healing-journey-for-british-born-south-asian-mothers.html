Introducing: Rooted & Rising

A 6-week guided coaching programme for South Asian British-born mothers who are ready to:

  • Understand and break cultural emotional patterns
  • Reconnect to their true identity

  • Mother from calm, clarity, and conscious choice

All while being seen, supported, and celebrated by women who get it.
