The human side of work

Every workplace runs on people

Managers are expected to support everyone else, often without much support themselves. Teams are juggling pressure and change. Good people quietly lose confidence.

Good policies, processes and management systems matter. Alongside them, people often benefit from time and space to think, a few practical tools and someone outside the organisation to help them find their own way forward.

Energy Leadership

What happens in a session

My sessions are warm, practical, interactive and reflective. Depending on the group, they may include coaching questions, discussion, guided mindfulness, personal reflection and practical exercises.

Everything is adapted to the audience, and nobody is ever pressured to share anything personal.

How do you show up when the pressure is on?

The Energy Leadership Index (ELI) is an attitudinal assessment that helps people explore their perspectives, reactions and patterns, particularly under stress. For managers, leaders and anyone navigating a challenge, it offers a clear, practical starting point for choosing how they want to respond.

The ELI is a specialist tool I use where it fits, usually in coaching and leadership work. It isn't part of every workshop.
