The Mindfully Priya difference

More than a moment of calm

I believe workplace wellbeing is about more than taking a break from a busy day. It's about helping people understand themselves better: how they respond to pressure, what affects their confidence, what matters to them and how they show up for themselves and others.

My approach combines professional coaching with mindfulness and practical reflection. I create space for people to pause, get curious and see things from a different perspective, before exploring what they might want to do differently.

Whether I'm working with an individual, a leadership team or a room full of employees, my aim is the same: for people to leave feeling heard, with greater awareness and something useful they can take into everyday life.

I come from the corporate world myself, so I understand the pressures your people are under.

  • Midlife and career transitions, including menopause at work and changes in personal or professional identity.
  • Returning to work after maternity leave or another extended absence.
  • Stepping into leadership, changing career or rethinking what sustainable success looks like.
Coaching for maternity returners

Coming back after maternity leave is a pivotal moment for the person and for the organisation. Without support, capable people can lose confidence and question their place long before they say anything.

I work with returners before and after the return: what they want this phase of their career to look like, how they hold their professional identity alongside a new one, and how they handle the first demanding months with some steadiness. Nine sessions is my usual recommendation, four before the return and four or five afterwards.

About the Energy Leadership Index

The Energy Leadership Index (ELI) is an attitudinal self-assessment, available through iPEC-certified coaches. It looks at how someone tends to respond to everyday situations and how that shifts under pressure. It is a tool for reflection and is not a diagnosis or a measure of performance.

It takes about 20 minutes online, followed by a 60 to 90 minute personal debrief with me and an action plan. Organisations use it for individual leaders, for whole teams who want shared language, and as a 360 that adds colleagues' perspectives.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you deliver sessions online?

Yes. I work online with teams and individuals throughout the UK, and offer in-person delivery by arrangement, particularly in South London and Croydon.

Can we start with one wellbeing session?

Absolutely. A single interactive session can be a useful starting point. We can also explore a series if ongoing support would be more valuable.

Do you tailor sessions?

Yes. We begin with your priorities and the people attending, then agree a suitable focus, format and level of interaction.

Is individual coaching confidential?

Yes, within clearly agreed professional and legal limits. The organisation and the participant agree the purpose of the coaching and any reporting arrangements in advance. What someone shares in a session is not passed to their employer.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching and mindfulness are not therapy or medical treatment. If something a participant brings would be better supported elsewhere, I will say so and point them towards the right help.

Do you offer ELI assessments?

Yes, where appropriate. The Energy Leadership Index explores attitudes and responses, including under pressure. I can talk you through whether it would be useful for an individual or a leadership team.

How much does it cost?

It depends on group size, duration, preparation and whether the work is online or in person. Get in touch and I'll send you a tailored proposal.
