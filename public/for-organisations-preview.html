The human side of work

Looking after people isn't always straightforward

Every organisation has different priorities, yet many of the human challenges are surprisingly familiar. You may recognise some of these.

Managers need support too

Managers are often expected to support everyone else, without always having support themselves. They carry difficult conversations, other people's worries and their own workload, often with little reassurance.

Pressure and overwhelm

Busy minds, competing demands and little space to reflect before reacting.

Navigating change

New roles, restructures, returning to work and shifting expectations can unsettle even the most capable people.

Connection and confidence

People do their best work when they feel heard, valued and confident enough to contribute.

Not every challenge needs another policy or presentation. Sometimes people need space, useful tools and a fresh perspective, so they can respond more consciously and move forward.

Meet Priya

More than a moment of calm

I'm Priya Khan, an ICF-accredited and iPEC-certified coach, Energy Leadership Index Master Practitioner and mindfulness practitioner. Before coaching, I spent years in the corporate world, so I know first-hand the pressure people carry at work, and what it costs in energy, confidence and clarity.

I believe workplace wellbeing is about more than taking a break from a busy day. It's about helping people understand themselves better: how they respond to pressure, what affects their confidence and how they show up for themselves and others.

My approach blends professional coaching with mindfulness and practical reflection. Whether I'm working with one person or a room full of employees, my aim is the same: for people to leave feeling heard, with greater awareness and something useful they can take into everyday life.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you deliver sessions online?

Yes. I work online with teams and individuals throughout the UK, and offer in-person delivery by arrangement, particularly in South London and Croydon.

Can we start with one wellbeing session?

Absolutely. A single interactive workshop is the most common starting point. We can explore a series if ongoing support would be more valuable.

Do you tailor sessions?

Yes. We begin with your priorities and the people attending, then agree a suitable focus, format and level of interaction.

Is individual coaching confidential?

Yes, within clearly agreed professional and legal limits. The organisation and the participant agree the purpose of the coaching and any reporting arrangements in advance. What someone shares in a session is not passed to their employer.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching and mindfulness are not therapy or medical treatment. If something a participant brings would be better supported elsewhere, I will say so and point them towards the right help.

How much does it cost?

It depends on group size, duration, preparation and whether the work is online or in person. The introductory conversation is free, and afterwards I'll send you a tailored proposal.
