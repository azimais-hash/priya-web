Energy Leadership Index

Unlock Your Potential with the Energy Leadership Index (ELI) Assessment

The Energy Leadership Index (ELI) Assessment is one of the most innovative assessment tools on the market. Featured in Forbes as a must-have for improving corporate leadership, this assessment actually has the profound potential to create amazing insight and fast track change in ALL areas of your life. For example, the ELI can help you uncover how you react to stress and challenges, so you can develop a plan to become a better leader.

Every day, our mindset shapes how we lead, collaborate, and make decisions. The Energy Leadership® Index (ELI) Assessment uncovers the hidden patterns behind how you show up at work, especially under stress, and helps you shift from reactive to empowered.

Read more

Why Energy Matters

  • Beyond personality tests. Unlike static personality assessments, the ELI looks at your attitudes and perceptions and shows how they change in different situations.
  • Seven levels of leadership. You'll see where you spend most of your energy, from stressful, draining states to those that inspire creativity and performance.
  • Real business impact. Leaders who harness constructive ("anabolic") energy see better team engagement, greater innovation, and stronger results.
  • Quick online assessment. Spend about 20 minutes answering questions about how you think and react in everyday situations.
  • Personalised debrief. You'll have a 60-90-minute one-on-one session with me, a certified Energy Leadership practitioner, to explore your results and identify growth opportunities.
  • Action plan. Together we'll map out simple shifts you can make to lead with more clarity, confidence, and purpose.

How It Works

  • Individual Insight: Perfect for executives and managers who want to raise self-awareness and sharpen their impact.
  • Team Experience: Give your whole team the language and tools to improve communication, accountability, and trust.
  • 360° Perspective: Add feedback from colleagues and stakeholders for a complete picture of your leadership energy.

Coaching for Corporate Women in Leadership

Reports shows that less than a quarter (24%) of women go back to full-time after having children, and of that 24%, 79% ended up leaving anyway due to not being able to maintain a full-time role alongside having a baby.

This statistic highlights a critical issue: businesses are losing talented, experienced women due to a lack of support in balancing work and family life. The cost of recruitment, retention, and lost expertise is significant, yet there is a solution - one that benefits both mothers and organisations.

Coaching for Mothers Returning from Maternity Leave

Supporting Businesses & Mothers for a Smooth Transition Back to Work

Returning to work after maternity leave is a pivotal moment, one that affects both mothers and businesses. Without the right support, many talented women struggle to balance work and family life, leading to stress, burnout, and even career breaks.

Benefits for Businesses & Organizations:

  • Retain Top Talent - Reduce turnover by providing support that helps mothers stay and thrive.
  • Boost Employee Well-being - Lower stress and increase confidence in returning mothers.
  • Enhance Productivity - A supported employee is more engaged, focused, and efficient.
  • Strengthen Workplace Culture - Foster inclusivity and position your company as family-friendly.
  • Reduce Sickness & Burnout - Prevent exhaustion by equipping mothers with the right mindset and tools.

How This Coaching Supports Mothers:

  • Gain confidence, overcome self-doubt and imposter syndrome to feel empowered in your role.
  • Work-Life Balance, understanding how to manage your career and motherhood without feeling overwhelmed.
  • Manage Emotional Resilience & Stress using mindfulness tools to handle challenges with calm and clarity.
  • Redefining Your Professional Identity by navigating career growth while embracing your new role as a mother.

We provide specialist coaching to help mothers transition back into the workplace with confidence and clarity.

We recommend at least 9 sessions, 4 sessions before returning to work and 4-5 sessions after maternity leave.
