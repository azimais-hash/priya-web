Who this is for

You know something needs to change, but what you have built is too valuable to change carelessly.

Private coaching for experienced women navigating a significant career, identity or life transition. Most are senior, established and quietly at a crossroads. Some arrive at a different kind of turning point altogether, and the work is the same: somewhere serious to think before you decide.

Does any of this sound familiar?

Not a crisis, and not a breakdown. Something quieter than that.

You're good at what you do. You always have been.

But there's a quiet voice underneath the competence lately, one that says: This doesn't feel quite like mine anymore.

You haven't lost the plot. You're not falling apart, and it's not ingratitude.

You've just outgrown something, a role, a way of showing up, a version of yourself you built for a life that no longer fully fits who you are, that brings you joy, purpose, excitement, peace.

The question underneath it all, the one you don't always say out loud is: Who am I, on the other side of all of this?

You might be feeling...

  • Successful on paper, and privately questioning whether you still want the career you built
  • Exhausted in a way that sleep and holidays don't seem to fix
  • So good at being who everyone needed you to be that you have lost the thread back to yourself
  • Pulled between your heritage, your roles, and who you actually want to become
  • Wondering, carefully, who you are on the other side of all of this

If you nodded at even one of those, you're in the right place.

The work itself

The questions this is for.

Most women arrive with one of these sitting underneath everything else, and nowhere private enough to think it through.

  • Do I still want the career or business I worked so hard to build?
  • Am I genuinely ready for change, or am I simply exhausted?
  • What do I want the next ten or fifteen years to look like?
  • How do I stop organising my life around what everyone else needs from me?
  • How do I change this without destroying the parts of my life that are still good?
  • What is my own judgement saying, underneath duty, fear, habit and expectation?
How the six months work

The backpack

What does coaching actually feel like?

Think about a backpack you've been carrying for years.

At first, you didn't even notice it. You simply adjusted to the weight. Responsibilities, expectations, other people's needs, old beliefs about who you 'should' be, they were added gradually, and you got stronger at carrying them. You kept walking.

But over time, the weight becomes normal. The tight shoulders, the quiet exhaustion, the sense that everything feels heavier than it should, that just becomes your life.

Coaching offers a rare moment to stop walking and gently take the backpack off.

You get to open it. To look inside. To notice what you've been carrying - the guilt, the pressure, the fear of disappointing others, the stories you've been telling yourself.

Some things still belong to you. Some were never yours to carry. And others you're finally ready to put down.

I don't empty the bag for you. I don't decide what stays or goes.

I simply sit beside you while you sort through it.

When you stand up again, the road hasn't changed. But you feel lighter, and that changes everything.

How I work

A different kind of thinking partner.

I'm an ICF-accredited coach, trained through iPEC in Energy Leadership and Transition Dynamics, and a certified mindfulness practitioner through the British Mindfulness Institute.

Three disciplines, brought together so that whatever sits underneath the surface, we have the means to reach it. A structured, intelligent, deeply human process rather than a conversation that wanders.

We begin with the Energy Leadership Index, an attitudinal assessment recommended by Forbes and available only through iPEC-certified coaches. It shows how you show up day to day, and how that shifts under pressure. Your first session is the debrief.

Mindfulness runs underneath all of it, and its job is simple: it improves the quality of the decision.

Frequently asked questions

What actually happens in this work?

A supportive, non-judgemental, forward-looking space to examine where you are, what feels out of balance, and what you want next. I blend mindfulness and professional coaching to help you see your own patterns clearly and make decisions you can stand behind, in your work, your life and your relationships.

Who is your coaching work for?

Experienced women, usually somewhere between their mid-forties and late fifties, who have reached a real crossroads in their career or their life and want to think it through privately before they decide. I also work with organisations who want to keep and develop experienced women through exactly those transitions.

What's the best way to book a free session?

You can book a free 30-minute session through my booking page or email me at hello@mindfullypriya.com. It's a relaxed, no-pressure conversation where you can share what's on your mind, explore what you'd like support with, and see whether coaching with me feels like the right fit for you.

Can I work with you online if I'm not in London or the UK?

Yes, absolutely. I coach clients all over the world through secure online sessions. Whether you're based in Europe, the US, or Asia, we can easily find a time that works for your time zone.

Online sessions are just as reflective and transformative as in-person coaching, and allow you to access support wherever you are.

What happens in a coaching session with you?

You can expect a calm, supportive space where you're invited to pause, reflect, and speak honestly without judgement. Together, we'll explore what's feeling heavy, unclear, or stuck, and use mindfulness and coaching tools to build awareness and gentle next steps that feel true to you.

How long does coaching usually take?

It really depends on what you want support with. Some people feel clearer after a few sessions, while others prefer a longer piece of work for deeper change. You can begin with a free call, and then decide what feels right for you based on your goals, pace, and what's happening in your life.

What's the difference between therapy and life coaching?

Therapy works with what has happened to you, often clinically and often with the past. Coaching works with what you decide to do from here. I am not a therapist and this is not treatment, and if what you bring would be better served by therapy I will say so.

My approach uses mindfulness and reflection to understand the patterns you are acting from, then practical steps that hold up in the real world.

How is your coaching approach different from other life coaching?

I blend mindfulness, values-based inquiry, and Energy Leadership™ coaching to support you from the inside out. That means you get a gentle, grounded space to slow down, notice what's really going on, and take clear, practical steps forward. It's ideal if you want change without feeling pushed or overwhelmed.

What kinds of programmes and workshops do you offer?

I offer a variety of wellbeing programmes and workshops focused on mindfulness, identity, balance, burnout and emotional resilience.

Sessions are designed to meet people where they are from new parents and professionals to community groups and schools.

I also create bespoke programmes for organisations or groups who want something tailored to their specific needs, themes, or goals.

How can my organisation or team work with you?

I partner with businesses and organisations to deliver bespoke wellbeing and coaching solutions that strengthen emotional resilience, mindful leadership, and workplace connection.

These can include one-off sessions, multi-week programmes, or full wellbeing initiatives designed around your company culture.

Whether your focus is stress management, leadership development, or supporting employees returning to work, each session is practical, inclusive, and rooted in mindfulness.
