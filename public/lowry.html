For Lowry Legal clients

Separation changes more than your relationship.

It can affect your confidence, routines, family life, sense of self and what you thought the future would look like.

Transition coaching helps you make sense of the change, understand what you need now and begin thinking about what comes next.

A complimentary one-hour coaching session for Lowry Legal clients going through separation or divorce.

BOOK MY FREE HOUR

Support for the part that isn’t legal

  • Your solicitor has the legal side.
  • Family and friends may have opinions.
  • Children may need reassurance.
  • Your ex has their own perspective.
  • Life still keeps moving.

So where do you get to work out what all of this means for you?

That is where transition coaching can help.

What could you get from the hour?

  • Clear your head

    Talk through what is going round in circles and make things feel more manageable.

  • Feel heard

    Say what you really think and feel without worrying about anyone else’s reaction.

  • Make sense of how you’re feeling

    Understand the mix of emotions that can come with such a big change.

  • Hear your own voice again

    Step away from everyone else’s opinions and focus on what you need.

  • Feel less lost about what comes next

    Begin thinking about your next step without having to work everything out at once.

What happens?

We meet online for one hour, there is nothing to prepare and no need to know exactly what you want to talk about. We start with whatever feels most important for you. I will listen, ask questions and help you untangle what is going on. Where useful, I may also bring in simple mindfulness or coaching tools to help you feel more grounded and see things differently.

BOOK MY FREE HOUR

When you are ready to look ahead

The questions may begin to change.

  • Who am I now?
  • What matters to me?
  • What do I want life to look and feel like?

There is no rush to have the answers. Transition coaching can help you reconnect with yourself and begin thinking about the future in a way that feels right for you.
