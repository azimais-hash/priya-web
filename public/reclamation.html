The Reclamation Programme™

You know something needs to change. What you've built is too valuable to change carelessly.

Private transition coaching for experienced women at a significant crossroads in work or life.

  • Sessions12 sessions
  • Rhythm2 per month
  • Length6 months
  • Format1:1 private

You are not in crisis. From the outside, very little looks wrong. You are good at what you do, you show up for your work, your family, your team, your community, and you hold a great deal together, usually without anyone realising quite how much.

Underneath the competence, though, something has shifted. Quietly, gradually, without a single dramatic moment you could point to. A faint sense that the life you’ve built, the one that looks right from the outside, doesn’t quite fit the woman you are now.

“Maybe having a fulfilled life, but not feeling fulfilled within it.”

You might recognise this:

  • Privately wondering whether you still want the career or the business you worked so hard to build
  • Weighing a change you haven't said out loud yet: stepping up, stepping sideways, reducing, leaving, starting something
  • Tired in a way that sleep and holidays don't seem to fix, and unsure whether that is a reason to change or a reason to wait
  • So practised at being who everyone needs you to be that your own preference has gone quiet
  • Aware that the next ten or fifteen years are being decided now, by default or on purpose

If you nodded at even one of those, you’re in exactly the right place.

The questions this work is for

Most women arrive with one of these somewhere underneath everything else. We take them seriously, and we take our time.

  • Do I still want the career or business I worked so hard to build?
  • Am I genuinely ready for change, or am I simply exhausted?
  • What do I want the next ten or fifteen years to look like?
  • How do I stop organising my life primarily around what everyone else needs from me?
  • How do I make a change without destroying the parts of my life that are still good?
  • What is my own judgement saying beneath duty, fear, habit and expectation?

Identity displacement

What you're experiencing has a name. It's identity displacement.

Over years, sometimes decades, of building a life around other people's needs, expectations, and definitions of success, the person who was doing all of that building quietly stepped aside. A highly competent performance took her place and for a long time, nobody noticed, including you.

The roles weren't wrong. The achievements weren't wrong, but somewhere along the way, the life stopped feeling authored by you.

Why this matters

This isn't a problem you can think your way out of, or solve with a new plan, a promotion, or a holiday. It requires something different: a structured, intelligent, deeply personal process of returning to yourself. Space to recognise what has been yours all along, releasing what never was, and redesigning your life from the inside out.

What the six months are for

  • Discernment before a decision that matters. Room to think properly, at the point where getting it wrong would be expensive.
  • A confidential space outside your own life. Somewhere that sits apart from work, family and friendship, with nothing riding on what you conclude.
  • Clarity about what is actually yours. Separating your own wants from the expectations, roles and habits you inherited along the way.
  • A way to change what needs changing. Without assuming that reinvention has to mean dismantling what is still good.
  • Confidence in your own judgement. So the next decision, and the one after that, can be made without a second opinion.

Five phases over six months

Over six months and twelve sessions we work through five phases. Each one builds on the last, and each one is designed to give you something you can carry forward: language, practices, and a clarity about yourself that holds once the sessions end.

  1. RecogniseSee what has always been true
  2. ReleaseLet go of what no longer fits
  3. ReclaimReturn to who you really are
  4. RedesignChoose your next chapter
  5. RiseRoot the new self and live it

How the six months are structured

  • Twelve private sessions, two a month, across six months. The fortnightly rhythm is deliberate: it gives the thinking time to settle between conversations, and it fits a full diary.
  • We begin with the Energy Leadership Index assessment, which shows how you show up day to day and how that shifts under pressure. Your first session is the debrief.
  • Between sessions, reflection work that takes minutes rather than evenings.
  • Sessions are one to one and entirely private, online or in person in South London.

Who it's for

  • You're professionally established and, by most measures, still doing well
  • Something has made the question harder to ignore: a plateau, a promotion, a restructure, a parent who needs you, menopause, children becoming independent, a business you're no longer sure you want
  • You have enough autonomy and resources to act on what you decide
  • You want somewhere private and rigorous enough to think properly, rather than encouragement

Who it isn't for

  • Coaching is not therapy or medical treatment. If you're living with a diagnosed condition, depression, or a stretch of real distress, therapy or your GP is the right support, and I'll say so plainly. In a crisis, call 999 or go to A&E.
  • If you'd like to be handed a plan and told what to do, another format will serve you better
  • If nothing has changed and nothing needs deciding, this is more than you need right now

Before you ask

Why is there no price on this page?

Because the right answer depends on what you need. Six months of private work is a significant commitment of money and attention, and I would rather tell you what it costs once I understand what you are facing, and say plainly if something smaller would serve you better. You will have a clear figure on the call, and there is no expectation that you decide on it.

What if I am not sure this is the right thing?

That is a reasonable place to start from, and the call is where we find out. If it is not right, I will tell you, and you will still leave with a clearer view of what you are weighing up.

Is any of this shared with anyone?

No. What you say stays between us. I do not use client stories in my writing or my marketing.
