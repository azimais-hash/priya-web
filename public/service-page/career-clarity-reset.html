Career Clarity Reset

Find your direction with confidence and calm

  • Sessions1 hr
  • WhereOnline calls

Are you feeling lost in your career... even though on the outside you look like you're doing "fine"?

Maybe you’re…

  • Questioning your direction
  • Successful on paper but emotionally unfulfilled
  • Returning to work or considering a career change
  • Recovering from burnout or redundancy
  • Craving meaning, confidence and clarity, not just another job

You don’t need more pressure or advice.

You need space to reconnect with yourself and reset your direction. This 6-week coaching journey supports women at any stage of life to find clarity, confidence and direction, without burnout, people-pleasing or overwhelm.

This is not traditional career coaching focused only on CVs or productivity.

This is deep identity work combined with practical coaching, helping you understand:

  • Who you are now
  • What truly matters to you
  • What's blocking your confidence
  • What direction fits your energy, values and life stage

So that you can move forward feeling empowered - not overwhelmed.

This coaching journey will help you:

  • Find clarity on your next career steps
  • Rebuild confidence and self-trust
  • Release people-pleasing and fear-driven decisions
  • Regulate anxiety and overwhelm around work
  • Reconnect to your strengths, purpose and values
  • Create a career direction that supports your emotional wellbeing

What’s included

  • 6 x Weekly 75-minute 1:1 coaching sessions (online)
  • Career Identity & Values workbook
  • Guided mindfulness recordings for clarity and calm
  • WhatsApp support between sessions
  • Personalised career direction map

My approach

I’m an ACC ICF-accredited Mindfulness & Career Coach, trained in Energy Leadership coaching (iPEC) and mindful practices.

This means our work together focuses not only on goals… but on:

  • Emotional safety
  • Nervous-system regulation
  • Identity transformation
  • Confidence and empowerment

True career clarity comes when you feel calm, connected, and courageous, not rushed or pressurised.
